Data Flow & Purpose: Explain what kind of data is processed at this level and 
how it supports decision-making

1. Strategic Level (ESS) 
- Most managers use this system to show business trends, graphs and dashboard this sytems is used for a long term period 
- it also helps managers to know which product they can continue selling as they compare graphs for the past few years
- They also uses this to compare their prices with their competitors to raise their market value.
eg if the competitor sells a product for more price the company can sell it for less price/ make promotions to attract more customers 


2. TACTICAL LEVEL (MIS/DIS)
- Its purpose is for managers to check the perfomance of the companty using the TPS data 
- Most companies use this for short term period as managers uses this reports daily, weekly and monthly depending on how and when they want to check the reports 

3. OPETRATIONAL LEVEL (TPS) 
- Its purpose is to handle daily transactions such as payments made to the company and stock counting 
- it records accurate data 
eg if a customer buys a product at a certain time the TPS will record the exact time when the product was purchased and which date it was purchased and also the accurate price

1. IT Infrastructure Diagram

2. IT Landscape Diagram
- This mostly shows what programmers make

- Unlike IT INFRASTRUCTURE this doesnt use phyical hadware instead it uses systems and their funtions 
- eg a company using excel to record thier monthly sales
